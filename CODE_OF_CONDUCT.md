# Community Rules

Be respectful and helpful.

People of every background and skill level are welcome here. Discuss ideas without attacking the person who shared them.

## Good behavior

* give clear and useful feedback
* accept that people may disagree
* help new contributors learn
* credit other people's work
* keep discussions related to the project

## Not allowed

* harassment or threats
* hate speech
* personal attacks
* spam
* sharing private information without permission

Serious or repeated problems may lead to comments being removed or accounts being blocked from the project.
